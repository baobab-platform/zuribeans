# ZuriBeans botanical identity — vector candidate

Status: **User-selected artwork, vector reconstruction for review** (not trademark-cleared or production-switched).

## Provenance
Reconstructed as native SVG paths from the selected ZuriBeans coffee-bean/leaf concept in the Nabhold Group Africa identity board, followed by the user's explicit direction to remove **COMMODITIES**. This is a hand-authored vector interpretation, **not an exact tracing** of source pixels. The previously generated transparent PNG contained visible generation artifacts and is intentionally not committed as a master.

## Assets
- `zuribeans-botanical-mark.svg`: standalone leaf/seed emblem, original SVG paths.
- `zuribeans-botanical-lockup.svg`: horizontal emblem plus uppercase ZURIBEANS only.
- `zuribeans-botanical-stacked.svg`: stacked alternative for editorial and print applications.

All SVGs are static, with no external resources, scripting, raster image references, filters, or embedded fonts. Wordmark text remains an SVG text element with a standard sans-serif fallback: **typeface-dependent rendering is still possible**. A production logo master should convert approved final lettering to outlined paths and undergo design approval.

## Brand palette
ZuriBeans retains its own estate identity: dark leafy green `#0F2C24`, leaf green `#2E5A4C`, trade gold `#C9A227`, and the Nabhold-family gold accent `#FCA300` (plus ivory `#FFFDF8`). Do not replace these with Nabhold parent black/gold or force a shared subsidiary emblem.

## Integration and release
The existing `public/brand/zuribeans-mark.svg` and its live consumers are deliberately unchanged. The brand review must check fidelity, legibility at 24/32/40/48 px, light/dark surfaces, accessibility, and proprietary mark clearance before switching the site header, favicon, or other estate assets. The source concept's white elements are adapted for light-surface legibility with controlled green/gold fills.

## Approval
The **brand concept** has user approval; this **vector reconstruction** has not yet been independently approved. No claims of trademark ownership or vector-perfect pixel fidelity.
